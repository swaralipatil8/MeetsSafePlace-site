Little Things That Matter

Tiny moments, big warmth — sticky notes from our diary.

Relax.

Your gentle reminder whenever I feel anxious. Your one word makes everything feel lighter.

AI

I call you 'AI' because you’re unbelievably good at coding — honestly, smarter than any machine.

Innocence

Despite being brilliant, you’re still so shy and innocent — and that’s what makes you my little baby.

Double-meaning jokes

They often fly over your head, and somehow that makes me laugh even more.

Late-night talks

Our conversations stretch into the night, becoming one of the most comforting parts of my day.

Encouragement

Whenever I call myself dumb, you remind me that I’m brave and never let me put myself down.

Safe place

Whenever I feel low, I come to you. You always listen and somehow make everything feel better.

Chess draw

You could have won easily, but you let the game end in a draw just so I wouldn’t lose.

Little apologies

You say sorry for even the smallest things — sometimes even when it’s actually my fault. That soft heart of yours is something I adore.

Afraid to lose me

Sometimes I can see how scared you are of losing me. And honestly, knowing that I mean that much to you makes me feel incredibly loved.

Cute aggression

You somehow manage to scold me when I make silly mistakes, and even your angry little lectures end up being cute to me.

A little possessive

You’re a little possessive and sometimes act like you don’t want to share me with anyone. It’s your cute way of saying, “I want you all to myself.”
