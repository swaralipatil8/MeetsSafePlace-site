A Letter for Meet

Open the envelope when you're ready. Take your time — this page is yours.

Dear Meet,

There are days when the world feels heavy and the future looks like a long, echoing hallway. I wish I could sit beside you in every one of those moments — to hold your hand, to make you tea, to remind you of the small steady things that keep you going.

I see how hard you try. I see the late nights, the quiet worries, the tiny victories you barely notice. You are learning, growing, and becoming — and none of that is wasted. Even when things feel uncertain, your kindness, curiosity, and courage are already more than enough.

When the noise gets loud, come back here. Breathe with the page, listen to the soft piano, and remember that you are not defined by a single day or a single result. You are loved for who you are — not for what you achieve.

Whenever life becomes too loud,
I hope this little place reminds you
how deeply loved you are.

Love,
Swarali ❤️

Take a breath. Stay a while.
